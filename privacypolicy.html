Privacy Policy — Tenpai

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Türkçe sürüm için tıklayın ↓

The short version: Tenpai is an offline, single-player game. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Tenpai is a mahjong-themed roguelike game developed and published by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it on Android devices.

2. Data we collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically, Tenpai does not collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or contacts
  • Your location
  • Photos, files, or media on your device
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
  • Microphone, camera, or any other sensor data

The app does not require an account, does not connect to our servers, and is fully playable offline.

3. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, Tenpai saves the following locally on your device only: game progress and saved runs, settings (such as language, volume, and accessibility options), statistics, achievements, and unlocked cosmetics. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

4. Purchases

Tenpai is a paid app sold through Google Play. The purchase transaction is handled entirely by Google. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's processing of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and third parties

Tenpai contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no social media integrations.

6. Children

Because Tenpai collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. The game contains no real-money gambling, no chat features, and no external links beyond this policy.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future update changes how the app handles data (for example, adding optional online leaderboards), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version is released.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: pikitapstore@gmail.com
